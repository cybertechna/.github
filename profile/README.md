## Overview

Cybertechna LLC is a cybersecurity advisory and services firm built on one idea: **security enables business**. Rather than treating security as an IT cost or a business worry, Cybertechna helps organizations turn it into a strategic accelerator that supports business continuity, regulatory compliance and brand preservation.

The firm argues that legacy, check-box defenses are no longer enough. It advocates "systemic security": an integrated posture that aligns technical controls directly with corporate strategy, championed "from the Boardroom to the Mailroom" and built in from idea through implementation.

## Education

Cybertechna is committed to educating organizations and executives on cybersecurity as a strategic asset, not just an operational expense. The goal is for leaders to understand how security drives growth, trust and resilience, so they can make informed decisions and treat it as a business capability. This is carried through the firm's blog and resources.

## What They Do

### Leadership & Strategy
- **Fractional CISO**: executive security leadership without a full-time hire
- **Board Advisory**: helping boards and executives understand and oversee cyber risk
- **Strategic Security**: integrating security into business strategy and delivery

### Risk & Compliance
- **Risk Assessments**: for regulatory audits or proactive visibility into where you stand
- **Risk Frameworks**: aligned with standards such as NIST and RMF
- **GLBA Compliance**: Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act compliance support
- **Policy Writing**: policies that give the organization a solid foundation

### Technical & Response
- **Incident Response**
- **Secure AI Integration**
- **Enterprise Governance**: policies, oversight and governance structures that show an organization is serious about security

## Approach

1. **Start with the foundation**: risk assessments, strategic architecture and organizational governance.
2. **Align security with strategy**: controls tied to business goals rather than bolted on afterward.
3. **Keep it continuous**: security integrated across the lifecycle, from the boardroom down.
